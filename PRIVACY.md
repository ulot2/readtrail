# Privacy policy

Archive keeps the pages you read on your own computer. It sends nothing anywhere.

Last updated: 9 September 2026.

## What is stored

For an article you read, Archive keeps:

- The address of the page
- The title
- The readable text of the article
- The date you first read it, and the date you last read it
- How many times you have opened it

It keeps nothing else. There are no images, no scripts, no cookies, no form
entries, and no passwords.

## Where it is stored

Everything goes into one database file inside Chrome's private storage for this
extension, on your computer. No website can read that file. No copy is made
anywhere else.

## What is never stored

Archive skips a page completely when any one of these is true:

- The window is incognito.
- The page has a password field on it.
- The site is on your denylist.
- Archiving is paused.
- You looked at the page for less than five seconds.
- The page is not an article.

## What is sent over the network

Nothing. Archive makes no network request of any kind. There is no server, no
account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. Every piece of
code it runs, including the database engine and the article reader, is inside the
extension. Nothing is downloaded while it runs.

This includes search. Related-word search reads statistics from your own archive
and nothing else, so a search never leaves your computer either.

The small site icons on the search page come from Chrome's own icon cache,
through the `favicon` permission. Showing one causes no request.

## Who can see your archive

You, on the browser where it is stored. Archive does not sell, share, or transfer
your data, because your data never reaches anybody who could.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Access to all websites | To read the article text of the page you are on. It is used for that alone |
| Storage | To remember your pause switch and your denylist |
| Offscreen | To run the database while the background script is stopped |

Archive asks for nothing else. It cannot read your history, your bookmarks, your
downloads, or your saved passwords.

## What you control

- **Search.** Click the toolbar button.
- **Delete.** Remove one page, one site, or everything older than a date, under Manage.
- **Export.** Write the whole archive to a file that is yours to keep.
- **Pause.** Stop archiving at any moment. The toolbar button shows a red badge while paused.
- **Block.** Name any site that must never be archived.
- **Remove.** Uninstalling the extension deletes the database with it.
